How should an AI agent behave?

The design object has moved. We used to design utterances. Now we specify behaviour — the timing, the restraint, the permission, the modality choice, the silence, and the moment of disclosure. The behaviour of the system is mostly not in the response.

Most failures are not model failures. They are failures of specification: nobody wrote down what the system should do in that moment, so it invented something — and the invention was plausible enough to ship.

The position If you cannot say what your agent should do when the conversation turns awkward, you have not designed it. You have delegated a thousand decisions to an implementation that will make them without you.

The six policies

  • 01 Language — what should I say?
  • 02 Conversation — when should I speak, listen, interrupt or wait?
  • 03 Modality — should I say it, show it or act?
  • 04 Agency — should I act or ask permission?
  • 05 Attention — what should I surface while I’m working?
  • 06 Representation — who am I when I interact on your behalf?
